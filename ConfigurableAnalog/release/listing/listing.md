# ConfigurableAnalog, Connect IQ store listing

App name: ConfigurableAnalog

Tagline (under 80 characters):
Analog face with weather, health and calendar pages, three skins, live weather.

Long description (under 2000 characters):

ConfigurableAnalog is an analog watch face for the fenix 8 family. Everything on the dial is data driven: three skins (Classic, Minimal, Sport) set colours, hands, textures and panels, and up to six pages hold the data you choose.

Pages. Hold the centre of the dial to cycle pages. Generic pages show five readouts from 34 sources, among them steps, heart rate, battery days, body battery, pulse ox, sleep score, recovery, VO2 max, humidity, wind, barometer, solar intensity, a second time zone and the next calendar event. Hand style can be set per page. Percentages get a rim gauge. The weather page shows the current condition as a large icon, the moon with its real phase and craters (computed on the watch), sunrise and sunset times, temperature, rain chance, UV index, pressure and a six hour pressure trend, with a star field at night. Discreet animated weather effects (rain, snow, wind, fog, thunder) play behind the dial while the watch is awake. The health page shows heart rate in the centre with stress, body battery, pulse ox and HRV around it, plus a small six hour trend chart of the metric you choose.

Hold any readout to open its native app. The on device watch face editor lets you pick a skin and the complication in each field without the phone; phone settings cover page types, slots, units, effects and refresh interval. A condensed typeface and a grey palette with pastel accents stay readable on MIP and AMOLED.

Battery: the dial is cached, only hands and values redraw each minute, the seconds hand uses low power partial updates where supported, and weather refreshes at most every 15 minutes.

Supported devices: fenix 8 Solar 51 mm, fenix 8 Solar 47 mm, fenix 8 47 mm and 51 mm, fenix 8 43 mm. Requires Connect IQ 5.1 or later.

Permissions: sensor history (body battery, stress, pulse ox, pressure), positioning (sun and moon times), complication subscriber (calendar event, HRV, opening native apps).
